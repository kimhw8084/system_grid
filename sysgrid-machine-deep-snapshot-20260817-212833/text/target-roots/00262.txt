# Changelog

## v2.0.2 — immutable history and quiet operator reporting

- Preserves every artifact under a run-identified, content-addressed Outbox history.
- Keeps the canonical product-review ZIP unchanged when a preflight or duplicate-run diagnostic is published.
- Adds a separate `UPLOAD_DIAGNOSTIC_TO_CHATGPT.zip` alias and `sg recover-artifact <run-id>` without product replay.
- Recognizes exact mutation post-state as an already-applied capsule and blocks duplicate execution before the engine starts.
- Reduces Telegram to at most three summary messages: start with full attempt detail, one high-level verification update, and final completion/failure.
- Retains all registered progress events, process metrics, and heartbeat telemetry locally for `sgs` and immutable evidence.
- Existing exact-engine, cross-ledger, recovery, no-replay, atomic publication, resource, and evidence guarantees remain unchanged.
