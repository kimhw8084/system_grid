# Changelog

## v2.1.5 — exact untracked-directory state repair

- Expands Git-collapsed embedded untracked directories into exact file-level state paths so immutable hash ledgers remain satisfiable.
- Excludes nested `.git` metadata, refuses symlinked or non-regular untracked content, and enforces a bounded file-count ceiling.
- Preserves the campaign/promotion verification architecture and every prior exact-engine, evidence, recovery, notification, and delivery guarantee.

- Adds `development_campaign` and `promotion_qualification` execution profiles while preserving one-command operator use.
- Development campaigns require declarative changed-path/test/view scope, retain cumulative uncommitted state, and cannot claim acceptance or commit/push.
- Promotion qualification remains the only profile permitted to perform the complete release gate, commit/push, remote equality, and acceptance.
- Preserves every artifact under a run-identified, content-addressed Outbox history.
- Keeps the canonical product-review ZIP unchanged when a preflight or duplicate-run diagnostic is published.
- Adds a separate `UPLOAD_DIAGNOSTIC_TO_CHATGPT.zip` alias and `sg recover-artifact <run-id>` without product replay.
- Recognizes exact mutation post-state as an already-applied capsule and blocks duplicate execution before the engine starts.
- Reduces Telegram to at most three summary messages: start with full attempt detail, one high-level verification update, and final completion/failure.
- Retains all registered progress events, process metrics, and heartbeat telemetry locally for `sgs` and immutable evidence.
- Existing exact-engine, cross-ledger, recovery, no-replay, atomic publication, resource, and evidence guarantees remain unchanged.
